\chapter{1919:No Prize Awarded}
\label{chap:chemistry1919}

No Nobel Prize in Chemistry was awarded in 1919. In accordance with the statutes of the Nobel Foundation, the prize money for the year was allocated to the special fund of the Chemistry prize section.

\section*{Chapter Summary}

The Nobel Committee for Chemistry did not award the prize in 1919, one of the years in which the regular award cycle was interrupted. Such gaps reflect the extraordinary circumstances of world events rather than an absence of noteworthy chemical research.
