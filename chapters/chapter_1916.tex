\chapter{1916:No Prize Awarded}
\label{chap:chemistry1916}

No Nobel Prize in Chemistry was awarded in 1916. The First World War, which had begun in 1914, interrupted the work of the Nobel Committees, and the award cycle remained suspended for several years.

\section*{Chapter Summary}

The Nobel Committee for Chemistry did not award the prize in 1916, one of the years in which the regular award cycle was interrupted. Such gaps reflect the extraordinary circumstances of world events rather than an absence of noteworthy chemical research.
