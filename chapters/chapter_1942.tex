\chapter{1942:No Prize Awarded}
\label{chap:chemistry1942}

No Nobel Prize in Chemistry was awarded in 1942, when the Second World War suspended the work of the Nobel Committees in Stockholm.

\section*{Chapter Summary}

The Nobel Committee for Chemistry did not award the prize in 1942, one of the years in which the regular award cycle was interrupted. Such gaps reflect the extraordinary circumstances of world events rather than an absence of noteworthy chemical research.
